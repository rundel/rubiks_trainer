    Learn the moves

    Each letter means one side of the cube. Hold the cube still and look at the front.

    • R right, L left, U up (top), D down (bottom), F front, B back.
    • A letter alone means turn that side clockwise, as if you were looking straight at that side.
    • A little dash (', say "prime") means turn it the other way.
    • A 2 means turn it twice (a half turn).
